# Scale-transfer review profile

Follow the shared contract in [record-reviews.md](record-reviews.md).

Read each cited primary source and bind the reported organism, strain, conditions and quantities to exact source locations. Check volume kind, compartment, units, denominator/basis, sampling context and uncertainty retained in the source literal. Missing reporting is a gap, not permission to calculate an implied number.

An observed scale-transfer claim needs actual reported cultivation conditions at the compared scales and source-supported endpoints. Experimental and simulated scale-down systems remain model evidence. A reported percentage difference may reference a control condition without inventing that control's absolute result. Inspect whether the compared endpoint, strain, media, sampling basis and experimental controls justify the source's comparability statement.

Check media at scale, OTR and kLa in their reported biological and physical scope. Do not promote a model target to an observed fermentation or treat a nominal vessel size as working broth volume. The source's calculations may be retained as reported calculations; new curator ratios or unit conversions are outside initial authoring.

For PISCES, verify the actual external resource metadata and matching SFID/URL, state context-only relevance, and preserve the links-only boundary. No flowsheet, unit-operation or TEA payload belongs here. A broad product/process similarity is not equivalence.

Review all changed records, their source projections and excluded candidates. Distinguish deterministic validation from scientific assessment. Confirmed findings become issues and require fixes and independent re-review before a user merge decision.
